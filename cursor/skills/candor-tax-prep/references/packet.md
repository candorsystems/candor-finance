# The packet

What the person hands their preparer, or keeps for their tax software. Give
it in chat; the records themselves come from Export in Candor Settings.

## Sections

1. **Scope.** Tax year, accounts covered, and months or accounts Candor could
   not see.
2. **Income.** Wages arrive with a W-2, so list only what the preparer may
   not have: side business deposits, 1099 work, rental income, interest from
   accounts without a form. Each with its total and count.
3. **Groups to review.** One line per group: what it is, the total where the
   grouping holds, the count, and its tag.
4. **Exceptions.** Mixed-use purchases, refunds that offset costs,
   reimbursed expenses, duplicates removed, records that cross the year line.
5. **Missing evidence.** Receipts, mileage, business purpose, written
   acknowledgments for larger gifts.
6. **Questions for the preparer.** Short and specific.

## Common groups

| Group | Look for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Side business income | Marketplace, client, and payment app deposits | Transfers between own accounts, refunds to customers |
| Business costs | Supplies, software, fees, advertising | Mixed personal use, refunds |
| Charitable gifts | Payments to charities | Gifts to people, purchases at charity events |
| Medical | Doctors, pharmacy, insurance not paid through payroll | Reimbursed costs, HSA-paid costs |
| Education | Tuition, required fees | Paid by a 529 or a scholarship |
| Estimated taxes | Payments to the IRS or state | Payments for an earlier year |
| Childcare | Daycare, after-school care | Dependent care FSA reimbursements |

Use tags that name the group (`tax-business-income`, `tax-charity`) so the
preparer and next year's packet can find them.

## Questions preparers usually ask

- Is the side business a separate entity, and are its accounts separate?
- How much of a mixed purchase was for business?
- Was a home office used regularly and only for work?
- Were any expenses reimbursed by an employer, an HSA, or an FSA?
- Do the larger gifts have written acknowledgments?
- Were estimated payments made, and for which year?

Ask the person only what changes the packet. Leave the rest as questions for
the preparer.
